# The Midnight Museum Mysteries — Teacher Quick Start

**Pupils:** Grades 3–5, A1. **Classroom:** one projector, one native-English-speaking teacher. **Duration:** exactly 50 minutes.

## Schedule

- 00:00–03:00 Opening: Professor Hoot announces the missing Moon Key.
- 03:00–10:00 Security Hall: camera check, flashlight, visitor portraits. Find small footprints.
- 10:00–17:00 Dinosaur Gallery: rebuild dinosaur, guess dinosaurs, find actions. Find grey fur.
- 17:00–24:00 Living Paintings: repair, listen to portraits, find 10 controlled differences. Find long tail.
- 24:00–31:00 Egyptian Chamber: hieroglyphs, dress mummy, maze. Find gold dust.
- 31:00–38:00 Lost & Found: memory, owners, evidence board. Find “I want to help” note.
- 38:00–45:00 Clock Tower: place clock numbers, repair grammar gears, identify mystery animal. Find Pip.
- 45:00–50:00 Reveal: Pip borrowed the key to repair the clock. Discuss clues and celebrate.

## Classroom language

“Look carefully.” “What can you see?” “Tell me in English.” “Can you say a full sentence?” “Good try! Look again.” “What clue have we found?” “What do you think happened?”

## Pace

Each task has 10 prompts. Select **Quick mode** (6 essential prompts) when running behind, or **Full mode** to play all ten rapidly. Do not skip a station's clue. Finish with five oral questions in the last five minutes.

## Answers

Answers, correct targets, and teacher-facing data are in `gameData.json` and the end of each activity section in `The_Midnight_Museum_Mysteries_Codex.md`. The browser game must hide them from pupils by default.
